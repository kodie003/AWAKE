Where we are

The road ahead

The curriculum and partnerships are designed, and we are preparing our first camp with one partner school. We are starting small so we can get it right before we grow.

Done

Design

Eight-module curriculum, camp rotations, safeguarding baseline and partner proposals.

Now

First camp

One partner school, around 25 to 30 girls aged 12 to 18, with a real before-and-after measurement.

Next

Weekly club

A weekly programme in Kentinkrono, Kumasi, with older girls trained as junior coaches.

Two-year goal

Twelve schools

Partner schools across Kumasi, Accra and Tamale, once the first camps show it works.

From the founder

"I kept seeing girls standing at the edge of the court, waiting for someone to pass them the ball. I wanted to be that someone."

Josephine Yeboah-Kodie grew up in Kentinkrono-Nsenie, Kumasi. She is a point guard, a youth basketball coach certified through Basketball For Good, and a Computer Engineering student at Ashesi University on a Mastercard Foundation scholarship. See her portfolio.
