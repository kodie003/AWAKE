The programme

How a camp works

Small groups, three stations, eight modules, and mentors who keep checking in after the camp ends.

How a camp works

Groups of four.
Three stations.

Girls split into small groups and rotate between stations, each led by a different coach or mentor. Nobody sits on the bench.

01

The court

Fundamentals first: BEEF shooting form (Balance, Eyes, Elbow, Follow-through), bounce passes and chest passes. Then small-sided games where every girl must touch the ball before anyone shoots.

02

The workbench

Hands-on renewable-energy builds with kits from STEM FOR ALL Ghana: girls assemble solar lanterns and power banks, no computer needed. When something does not light up, they find the fault themselves.

03

The circle

Mentorship from female athletes and young professionals, following our eight-module curriculum. Small groups, honest questions, and a follow-up check-in months after camp.

The curriculum

Eight modules

  1. 01Confidence

    Asking for the ball, and for help.

  2. 02Teamwork

    Winning as five, losing as five.

  3. 03Discipline

    Showing up when nobody is watching.

  4. 04Goal-setting

    One goal on the court, one in the classroom.

  5. 05Resilience

    A missed shot is information, not a verdict.

  6. 06Wellness

    Food, rest and a healthy body.

  7. 07Relationships

    Friends, family and saying no.

  8. 08Leadership

    Running a drill for the next girl.

How we measure

Numbers we can stand behind

  • Attendance log for every session, signed by the lead coach.
  • Before and after self-ratings on a 1 to 5 scale for confidence and teamwork.
  • Three-month check-in with every girl and her mentor.
  • Receipts matched to the budget, shared with every partner.

Safeguarding

Safe before day one

  • Two references and a signed code of conduct from every adult volunteer.
  • Never one-on-one: no adult is ever alone with a single girl.
  • Guardian consent for every girl, with separate consent for photos.
  • One named contact for any concern from a girl, parent or volunteer.
