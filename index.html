Why AWAKE

Girls are welcome to watch.

In most of the neighbourhoods we work in, the basketball court belongs to the boys. A few girls ask for the ball. Most never do, and after a while nobody expects them to.

The same thing happens with science. Many schools teach STEM from the textbook because there is no equipment for anything else, and many homes have no computer or reliable internet. A girl who has never built or broken anything starts far behind, in a world that is being rebuilt by technology.

AWAKE puts three things in one place: a court where every girl touches the ball, a workbench where she builds something real with her hands, and mentors who keep checking in after the camp ends.
